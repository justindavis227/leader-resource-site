PRY-02Listening Prayer
7 min

Prayer

Guide the room: 'For the next few minutes, stop talking to God and just listen. Get comfortable. Take three slow breaths. Ask one question—God, what do you want me to hear tonight?—and then be quiet.' Sit in real silence for 5 minutes; leader keeps time so no one has to watch the clock. Then invite anyone who wants to share a word, image, or nudge they sensed. No pressure—some will, some won't, and that's fine.
