Prayer

Give each leader a quiet minute to ask God one thing: what could I set aside this week to make more room for you? Then pray in pairs—name your simple fast to your partner (a meal, a day off the phone, coffee, whatever), and pray for each other to actually follow through and to meet God in the ache.
