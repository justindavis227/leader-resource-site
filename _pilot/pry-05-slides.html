Prayer

Each leader names their one—out loud if willing, silently if not. Then spend the bulk of tonight praying for those specific students by name: for softened hearts, for open doors, for one clear next step toward Jesus, and for the leader to have eyes and courage to be the worker God sends. This is the point of the huddle, not the warm-up.
