Prayer

Read Psalm 23 slowly, out loud, one verse at a time. After each verse, pause and let leaders pray that verse back to God—out loud or silently—over their own lives and their students. Leader models the first one so people catch the rhythm, then hands it off. Take your time; let the silences breathe.
