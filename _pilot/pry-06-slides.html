Prayer

Slow, unhurried gratitude round. Go around the circle two or three times—each leader thanks God out loud for one specific thing each pass: something in their own life, something in their group, something about this team. No 'right' answers, no pressure to be profound. Let it build. Close by thanking God together for the students you get to lead.
